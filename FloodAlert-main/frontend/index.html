🛟 Safety Instructions

Prepare & Stay Safe During Floods

📋 Before a Flood

  • Emergency Kit: Keep water, first aid, flashlights, batteries
  • Documents: Store passports, IDs, insurance papers in waterproof bag
  • Medications: Stock 2-week supply + prescriptions
  • Cash: Keep emergency cash at home
  • Contact List: Write down emergency numbers
  • Plan Route: Know evacuation routes & safe zones

⚠️ During a Flood

  • Move Up: Go to higher floors or roof immediately
  • Turn Off: Shut off gas & electricity if safe
  • Don't Drive: Never cross flooded roads (even shallow areas)
  • Stay Inside: Remain indoors away from windows
  • Listen Alert: Follow official radio/TV broadcasts
  • Help Others: Assist elderly & disabled neighbors if safe

🏘️ Where to Take Shelter

🏫

Schools & Colleges

Usually on higher ground, designed as evacuation centers

🏥

Hospitals & Health Centers

Medical support available, elevated locations

⛪

Temples, Mosques, Churches

Community shelters, often have supplies

🏛️

Government Buildings

Municipal offices, police stations, fire stations

🏨

Hotels on Higher Ground

Urban hotels often have emergency facilities

🏞️

Higher Elevations

Open spaces on hills, hilltops, elevated platforms

✅ After Flood Recedes

  • Check Safety: Inspect home for structural damage
  • Boil Water: Use only boiled/bottled water for drinking
  • Clean Thoroughly: Disinfect affected areas with bleach
  • Dispose Safely: Remove contaminated items properly
  • Get Medical Check: Watch for disease symptoms
  • Report Damage: Contact insurance & local authorities
